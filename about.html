HOW IT STARTED

I grew up on Sonic the Hedgehog, played on long car trips, and I've loved arcade games ever since. The name Avengis goes back to my first go at making games, writing scripts for GraalOnline.

These days I make the kind of games I want to play: quick to pick up, bright, bouncy, and full of little things that move. Every game gets its own art, music and menus, made for the world it's set in.

WHAT I'M MAKING

Rebounded is a brick breaker where the water is alive. It started on PC, where there's a free demo, and Rebounded: Pocket Edition brings it to Android, rebuilt for playing with your thumbs. Pocket Edition is out now on Google Play.

The Dugout is my next game: a roguelike for Android where a ball bashes its way down an abandoned mine for ore and buried treasure.

HOW I WORK

Players shape everything I make. Pocket Edition's testers, from over a dozen countries, sent notes that turned into real changes, from a clearer ball to smoother water, and I made a short video to thank them.

Pocket Edition is free to try, with one unlock for the full game. No adverts, nothing else to buy.
